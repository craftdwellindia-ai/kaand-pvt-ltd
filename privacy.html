Privacy policy

Kaand Uploader · Last updated 14 October 2026

Kaand Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of Kaand Pvt. Ltd. - the YouTube channel, the @kaand_pvt_ltd Instagram account and the Kaand Pvt. Ltd. Facebook Page - to publish their own videos and read how they perform. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload);
  • read the owner's channel name and the statistics of its own videos (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly), to decide when to post.

It does not access, collect or store any information about viewers or any other YouTube user.

Instagram and Facebook

With the owner's permission, granted through Meta's login screen, the app uses the Facebook and Instagram APIs to publish Reels and posts to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, read those posts' statistics (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves), and read comments left on them so the owner can review them. It does not collect information about followers or any other user beyond the comments on the owner's own posts.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens, the accounts' own post statistics and comments are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • YouTube: revoke the app at Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  • Facebook / Instagram: remove Kaand Uploader under Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites).
  • A stored comment: delete it on the platform, or email the address below with a link to it; it is removed from the owner's computer within 30 days.

All other locally stored data is deleted by removing the app's files.
