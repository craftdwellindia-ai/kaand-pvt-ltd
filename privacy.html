Privacy policy

Kaand Uploader · Last updated 12 October 2026

Kaand Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Kaand Pvt. Ltd. YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload);
  • read the owner's channel name and the statistics of its own videos (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly), to decide when to post.

It does not access, collect or store any information about viewers or any other YouTube user.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the channel's own video statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Locally stored data is deleted by removing the app's files.
