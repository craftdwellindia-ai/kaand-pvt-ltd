Privacy policy

SanityCheck app · Last updated 14 October 2026

SanityCheck is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the SanityCheck Facebook Page and the @sanity_check_ Instagram account to post that owner's own videos and images and read how they perform. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Meta's login screen, the app uses the Facebook and Instagram APIs to:

  • publish posts and Reels to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account;
  • read the statistics of those posts (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves), to decide what and when to post;
  • read comments left on the owner's own posts, so the owner can review them.

It does not collect, store or process any personal information about viewers, followers or any other Facebook or Instagram user beyond the comments on the owner's own posts, and those are kept only to show to the owner.

Where data is kept

Access tokens, post statistics and comments are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Meta's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and none is used for advertising.

Deleting your data

The only people whose data the app touches are the account owner and anyone who comments on the owner's posts.

  • To remove the app's access, go to Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) and remove SanityCheck.
  • To have a stored comment deleted, delete it on Facebook or Instagram, or email the address below with a link to it. Requests are handled within 30 days and the comment is removed from the owner's computer.
